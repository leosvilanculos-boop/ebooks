Refund Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026 · Applies to purchases on {{PUBLIC_ORIGIN}}

In short

  • Full refund within 14 days if your ebook was not delivered, the file is defective, or it is materially different from its description and we cannot fix it.
  • Other requests, including dissatisfaction, are reviewed case by case if made within 14 days of purchase.
  • Duplicate or unauthorised charges are always refunded once confirmed.
  • This policy does not limit any rights you have under consumer law.

1. Digital products

Our ebooks are digital goods delivered by download link or online access immediately after payment is confirmed. Payments are processed by our payment partner, Walled.

2. Refunds we always grant (within 14 days)

Contact us within 14 days of purchase and we will either fix the problem or give you a full refund if:

  • you did not receive access to the ebook you paid for;
  • the file is corrupted, incomplete or cannot be opened, and we cannot provide a working file;
  • the ebook is materially different from the description on our website.

3. Other requests (case-by-case review)

If you are not satisfied for another reason, you can still ask for a refund within 14 days of purchase. We review each request individually, taking into account the reason given and whether the content has been downloaded or accessed. We will explain our decision in writing.

4. Duplicate or unauthorised charges

If you were charged twice for the same order or did not authorise a payment, contact us. Confirmed duplicate or unauthorised charges are refunded in full, regardless of the 14-day period.

5. Your legal rights

Nothing in this policy excludes or limits rights you have under the consumer protection law of your country. Where the law gives you a right to cancel a purchase or to a remedy for faulty digital content, that right applies in addition to this policy.

6. How to request a refund

Email leosvilanculos@gmail.com with your order ID or the email used at checkout and a short description of the issue. We aim to respond within 5 business days.

7. Processing

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method through Walled. The time for the money to appear depends on your bank or card issuer, usually 5–10 business days. Access to the refunded ebook may be removed.

8. Chargebacks

Please contact us before opening a dispute with your bank — most issues can be resolved faster directly with us.

Questions? Email leosvilanculos@gmail.com with your order reference. We reply within 5 business days.
